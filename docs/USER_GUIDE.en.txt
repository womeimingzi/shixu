Shixu 0.1.0 — Windows desktop planner

GET STARTED
Extract the entire release ZIP into a folder of your choice.
Double-click the paw-icon executable (拾序.exe).
Keep the accompanying files and folders next to the executable.
No Node.js, Python, browser server, or account is required to use a release.

EVERYDAY USE
Type an item and press Enter to capture it. Click its title to edit details.
Daily check-in records today's progress; completing an entire task stops its reminders.
Weekly events are completed one occurrence at a time.
The current application interface is in Simplified Chinese.

BACKGROUND AND STARTUP
Closing the window keeps Shixu in the system tray by default.
Right-click the tray icon and choose Exit to stop the app and reminders.
Automatic startup is opt-in in Preferences. Startup launches silently into the tray.
Starting the app a second time brings the existing window forward.

REMINDERS
Daily progress: at the time selected for that task.
Timed events: 10 minutes before the start; date-only events: 09:00 that day.
Deadlines: 09:00 the day before and on the due date.
All schedule times currently use UTC+8 (Asia/Taipei).
The app cannot notify while Windows is asleep, powered off, or the app has exited.
Still-relevant reminders are checked after the computer resumes.
Windows Do Not Disturb and notification preferences may suppress toasts.

YOUR DATA
Records are saved in %APPDATA%\Shixu, separately from the executable.
Each save retains the preceding version as a backup.
Preferences offers JSON export, additive import, and an Open data folder action.
Browser preview records and desktop records do not continuously synchronize.
